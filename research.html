Research

Experimental nuclear and particle physics, atmospheric and cloud microphysics, scientific instrumentation, and detector development.

My interests also include computational data analysis and machine learning.

Research experience

Undergraduate Researcher

The College of New Jersey · January 2026–present

I analyze Jefferson Lab Neutral Particle Spectrometer data to reconstruct neutral-pion (π⁰ → γγ) events, developing C++ and CERN ROOT workflows for event selection, detector studies, and simulations. This work involves collaboration with Jefferson Lab and the Catholic University of America and high-performance computing for large datasets.

Adviser: Dr. Salina Ali

Undergraduate Intern

Thomas Jefferson National Accelerator Facility (Jefferson Lab) · May–August 2026

I supported experimental nuclear-physics research and detector/data-acquisition work for high-energy electron-beam experiments. I also participated in quality-assurance measurements of lead-tungstate calorimeter crystals, including optical-transmission studies.

Research Experiences for Undergraduates (REU) Researcher

The Catholic University of America · May–July 2026

I investigated neutral-pion electroproduction with liquid-deuterium data from Jefferson Lab Hall C. I applied numerical analysis and two-photon invariant-mass reconstruction to study candidate events and experimental backgrounds.

Adviser: Dr. Tanja Horn

Undergraduate Researcher

The College of New Jersey · April 2025–present

I apply cryogenic scanning electron microscopy (cryo-SEM) to characterize laboratory-grown ice-crystal morphology, contributing experimental observations relevant to cirrus-cloud microphysics and climate research.

Adviser: Dr. Nate Magee

Research Collaborator

Rutgers University, High Energy Experimental Physics (HEX) Group · June 2025–January 2026

I developed a neural-network analysis pipeline using Monte Carlo simulations for CMS supersymmetry searches and studied electroweak and R-parity-violating decay signatures using high-energy physics datasets.
