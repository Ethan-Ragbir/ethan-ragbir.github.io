Research

Atmospheric physics, particle physics, and the computational methods that help us study them.

Research experience

Ice morphology

The College of New Jersey · April 2025–present

I study ice crystal formation and morphology using scanning electron microscopy, investigating how temperature and vapor supersaturation affect crystal behavior. The work examines conditions between −45°C and −65°C to better understand cloud physics and climate modeling.

Advisor: Dr. Nathan Magee

Machine learning for particle physics

Rutgers University · June 2025–present

I work with the Rutgers high energy physics group on machine learning methods for analyzing data from the Compact Muon Solenoid (CMS) experiment at CERN. The work includes preprocessing collision data with ROOT and developing neural networks for particle identification and event classification.

Advisors: Dr. Alan Richards and Dr. Sunil Somalwar
